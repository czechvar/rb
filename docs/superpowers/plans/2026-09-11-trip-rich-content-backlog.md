## Files and assessment boundary

- [2026-09-11-trip-rich-content-backlog-events.csv](2026-09-11-trip-rich-content-backlog-events.csv): all 56 Event records, existing/missing structured fields, status and CMS links.
- [2026-09-11-trip-rich-content-backlog-occurrences.csv](2026-09-11-trip-rich-content-backlog-occurrences.csv): all 48 upcoming unenriched departures, locations, dates and review links.

Presence is a field-level content audit, not a visual or factual quality score. The audit checks overview, audience, learning, programme, comparison/options, authored venue text, logistics, package, active FAQs and heading overrides. Shared location/guide records, inherited legacy prose and custom blocks can supply additional visible content. A “missing” field means no content in that audited structured slot, not proof that equivalent wording is absent everywhere on the page. Sections such as comparison are optional and should only be populated where the trip needs them. No imagery is assessed or changed.
