Saalbach – One of the Biggest Ski Circuits in the Alps

Day 3 · Austria's Hidden Gem

Hochkönig – The König's Tour & Authentic Alpine Character

Discover the hidden gem of Austria's ski scene at Hochkönig, part of the Ski Amadé network. This resort combines three picturesque villages – Maria Alm, Dienten and Mühlbach – into one remarkable ski experience with 120 kilometres of interconnected slopes. Hochkönig's unique "König's Tour" lets you traverse the entire resort in a single day, with stunning views of the imposing Hochkönig massif. Less crowded than the big names, this is the perfect day for focused coaching work.

Day 4 · Legendary Terrain

Kitzbühel – Home of the World's Most Famous Downhill Race

Prepare for an unforgettable day at the legendary Kitzbühel, home to the world's most famous downhill ski race – the Hahnenkamm. With 233 kilometres of meticulously maintained pistes, Kitzbühel's varied terrain offers something for everyone: from gentle nursery slopes to the hair-raising Streif downhill course. Beyond the exceptional skiing, Kitzbühel enchants with its colourful, car-free town centre featuring frescoed buildings, exclusive boutiques and gourmet restaurants.

Day 5 · Grand Finale

Kitzsteinhorn – Salzburg's Only Glacier & the Perfect Send-Off

Conclude your Austrian ski tour with an ascent to Kitzsteinhorn – Salzburg's only glacier ski resort, offering snow-sure conditions year-round up to an altitude of 3,029 metres. This high-alpine winter playground provides 41 kilometres of pristine slopes with spectacular panoramic views across Austria's highest peaks. The summit station "Gipfelwelt 3000" features the National Park Gallery, Cinema 3000 and breathtaking viewing platforms. Advanced riders will love the five snowparks and natural half-pipes.

Location & Snow Conditions

The Salzburg Region is one of the most varied ski landscapes in the Austrian Alps – five connected resorts spanning from lake-level at Zell am See to high-altitude glacier skiing at Kitzsteinhorn (3,029m). With reliable snowpack from November through April and exceptional lift infrastructure, the Salzburgerland consistently delivers both quality and quantity. Fly into Salzburg (SZG) or Munich (MUC) airports.

[ MAP – Salzburg Region: Zell am See · Saalbach · Hochkönig · Kitzbühel · Kitzsteinhorn ]
  • Region: Salzburgerland & Tyrol, Austrian Alps
  • Elevation range: 762m (Kitzbühel base) – 3,029m (Kitzsteinhorn glacier)
  • Typical conditions: reliable groomed pistes Nov–Apr, glacier snow year-round
  • Combined terrain: 800+ km of pistes across all 5 resorts
  • Nearest airports: Salzburg (SZG) · Munich (MUC) – fly in to either

FAQ for This Trip

What skill level do I need for the Salzburg Shred Safari?
This trip is designed for intermediate to advanced riders comfortable on red and black groomed runs. You don't need to be a pro, but be ready to charge hard on the groomers with a high stoke level. See the rider level guide above.
Can I rent skis or snowboard on site?
Yes – equipment rental is available at all five resorts. We can recommend the best shops on request. Plus, the DOWN DEMO is included in your trip so you can test premium DOWN SKIS on the mountain before deciding.
Do I need to book accommodation separately?
Accommodation is not included in the trip price, but we're happy to help you find the right option – from traditional Gasthäuser to self-catering apartments. Get in touch and we'll point you to the best deals in the area.
Is a ski pass included?
The 5-day ski pass is not included in the price, but we'll guide you to the best regional pass options covering all five resorts. We know all the insider deals. All can be administered online from the comfort of your accommodation.
Is this trip suitable for families or solo travelers?
Absolutely – this trip is perfect for families, friend squads, and solo shredders. Riders 15+ are welcome; younger riders can join with parents. Around 70% of our guests travel solo and leave with new riding partners.
What dates can I choose for the trip?
The Salzburg Shred Safari runs from 29 November 2026 through 30 April 2027, with multiple departure dates available across the season. Contact us with your preferred dates and we'll confirm availability.
How do I travel between the five resorts each day?
We recommend renting a car for maximum flexibility – it's the most economical option and lets you explore at your own pace. Some hotels also offer shuttle services. We'll help you plan the logistics before arrival.
