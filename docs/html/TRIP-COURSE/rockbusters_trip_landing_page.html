Tactics

  • Long-term grade progression

The Venue

Rodellar, Spain

Rodellar is one of Europe's finest sport climbing destinations — a dramatic limestone canyon cut by the Río Mascún in the Spanish Pre-Pyrenees. The rock is featured, diverse, and magnificently sculptured.

Routes span 6a to 9a across three main sectors, with a concentration of 7s and 8s perfect for the camp's target range. The canyon's natural shade makes it climbable through the warmest parts of the day.

FAQ

Common Questions

What fitness level do I need?
You should be able to climb confidently at 6b outdoors on lead. The camp is designed for climbers working in the 6b–8a range. If you're unsure whether you fit, email us — we're happy to chat it through.
Do I need to bring my own gear?
Your own climbing shoes, harness, and belay device are ideal. All safety equipment (ropes, draws) is provided on the crag. Evolv and Singing Rock demo gear is available throughout the week.
Is the €200 Singing Rock voucher per person?
Yes — every participant receives a €200 Singing Rock voucher redeemable on their online shop. It's valid for 12 months from issue.
Can I do just one week and extend on-site?
The two-week format has a specific structure that builds on week one. If you're considering extending, book the two-week option from the start — we can't guarantee space for add-ons after arrival.
What's the cancellation policy?
Full refund up to 60 days before the camp. 50% refund 30–60 days before. No refund within 30 days, but your place can be transferred to another climber.
Are meals included?
Yes — breakfast, a packed lunch to take to the crag, and a communal dinner every evening. Dietary requirements are accommodated; please note them at booking.
