The Rockbusters Difference
Run By Climbers, For Climbers

Rockbusters isn't a travel agency that added climbing to its brochure. Every person who answers the phone, replies to your email, or matches you with a guide climbs themselves — and that changes the quality of every conversation.

01

We Speak Your Grade

Tell us you're "solid 7a, scared of 7b slab" and we know exactly what that means — no translation needed between what you say and what you actually want.

02

We Match Coach to Climber

We've climbed with our own guides. We know who's patient with nervous leaders, who pushes hard climbers harder, and who's brilliant on rock but terrible with beginners — so you get the right one.

03

We Organise Trips, Not Just Rooms

Logistics matter, but a good trip is built by people who understand approach times, seasonal conditions, and rest-day psychology — not just hotel inventory.

A Small Ask
We Might Be Climbing

Sometimes the person best placed to answer your question is on a route, not at a desk. If a reply takes a few hours instead of a few minutes, it's usually because someone's mid-pitch, coaching a group, or scouting the exact crag you're asking about.

We think that's a fair trade. It's the same reason our advice is worth listening to — we're not reading a spec sheet, we're describing routes and conditions we know first-hand.

We always get back to you — usually same day, always within 24 hours. WhatsApp is fastest if it's urgent.

Before You Ask
Common Questions

Who actually answers the phone?

One of our guides or coaches, always. We don't route calls through a booking platform or a non-climbing agent — the person you speak to understands what you're asking.

Why might it take a while to hear back?

Because the same people who guide your trip are often out guiding someone else's, or climbing themselves to keep their own standard sharp. We reply within 24 hours, always.

Can you help me choose between trips?

That's most of what we do. Tell us your grade, goals, and what's scared or excited you lately — we'll steer you to the right camp, course, or coach rather than the one with the biggest margin.

Do you speak English / Spanish / Czech?

Yes to all three, across both desks. WhatsApp us in whichever you're most comfortable in.
