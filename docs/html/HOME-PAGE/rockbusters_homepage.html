Quick answers

FAQ

Do I need outdoor climbing experience?

Not necessarily. We run trips and courses for all levels, from first-time outdoor climbers to those pushing 8c+. We'll match you to the right experience.

What level do I need to be?

From complete beginners to advanced sport climbers. Tell us where you are and we build the experience around your current level and goals.

What's included in trips?

Accommodation, professional guiding and coaching, equipment, area beta, and local knowledge. For local transport we connect you with fellow climbers to share rental cars and help organise the whole process. You cover flights, food, and personal expenses.

How big are groups?

We keep groups small — typically 6 to 10 people maximum — so every climber gets genuine individual attention and coaching time.

Can I come as a solo traveller?

Absolutely. Most of our climbers travel solo. We pair you with compatible partners from day one — it's one of the things we're known for.

Is coaching included on every trip?

Yes. Professional coaching and guiding is ongoing throughout every Rockbusters trip, camp, and course — not just on dedicated coaching days.
