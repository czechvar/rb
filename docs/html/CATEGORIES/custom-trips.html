Who We Are

For the groups our
other trips don't fit

Every other page on this site is a template — a fixed destination, a fixed format, a fixed date range, built for climbers who fit neatly into one category. Custom Trips exists for everyone else: the family with three different skill levels, the friend group who want half road trip and half performance coaching, the work team who need a week that's equal parts climbing and actually relaxing.

We start from the same building blocks as every other trip on this site — our destinations, our coaches, our accommodation options — and rearrange them entirely around your group. One shared goal or five different ones, one coach or several, a single venue or a full road trip between them. If we can build it for a set itinerary, we can build it for yours.

Most custom trips run for groups of four to twelve, though we've built trips as small as two and as large as twenty. Tell us who's coming and what they each want, and we'll draft an itinerary around it.

Frequently Asked

Before you book
a custom trip

What's the minimum group size?

Most custom trips work best from four people up, since that's where per-person costs start to make sense versus our set itineraries — but we've built trips for couples and pairs too. Tell us your group size and we'll confirm what's realistic.

Can we mix categories, like bouldering and a sport holiday?

Yes — that's most of what this category exists for. Split coaches by sub-group, alternate days by activity, or run two formats side by side at the same base. Tell us what each part of your group wants and we'll structure the days around it.

How do the camper vans work?

Our camper vans are fully equipped multi-berth vehicles, available in twos or threes for larger groups, and can be booked alongside any custom itinerary as your accommodation for part or all of the trip — ideal for multi-destination road trips.

How far in advance should we book?

For peak-season European destinations and camper van availability, three to six months ahead gives us the most room to work with. Shoulder-season and off-peak trips can often be built with less notice — ask us directly for your dates.

How is pricing worked out?

Every custom trip is quoted individually based on destination, group size, coach-to-climber ratio, and accommodation choice, so there's no fixed price list. Tell us your rough plan and we'll come back with a detailed quote.
