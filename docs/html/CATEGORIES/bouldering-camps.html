Who We Are

Coaches who still
chase the send

Rockbusters' bouldering camps grew out of a simpler idea than our roped trips: give climbers a forest full of boulders, a stack of pads, and enough days to actually finish the problems they came for. No belay logistics, no bolt-to-bolt pacing — just movement, skin, and the slow, satisfying grind of a proper project.

Every camp is led by a qualified bouldering coach who knows the field's classic circuits, its hidden testpieces, and exactly which problem to send you toward on a rest day. They spot your first attempts, call the beta you can't see from the ground, and know when to leave you alone with a project.

We cap every camp at ten climbers. Small enough that the coach remembers your project by name, big enough that there's always a spotter and someone to share a crash pad with.

Frequently Asked

Before you book a
bouldering camp

What grade do I need to boulder to join?

Our bouldering camps suit climbers comfortable on font 5/V2 and above, with circuits and projects adjusted daily to the group's range. Tell us your grade when booking and the coach will scope sectors accordingly.

Are crash pads included?

Yes — group crash pads, brushes, and a coach's spotting are always included. Personal kit like shoes and a chalk bag is yours to bring, though we can arrange rental at most camp hubs.

How is a bouldering camp different from a sport climbing holiday?

A bouldering camp is roped-free — no belaying, no bolts, just pads and problems in a single boulder field. Our sport climbing holidays instead use ropes and quickdraws on routes at a single sport crag.

Can I book just a few days instead of the full week?

Most camps run as a full 7 or 14-night package since accommodation and coaching are priced that way, but a few destinations offer a shorter add-on — ask when you enquire and we'll see what's possible for your dates.
