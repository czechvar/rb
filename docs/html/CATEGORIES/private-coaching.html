Who We Are

Coaching built around
one climber at a time

Every other program we run is built around a group — a shared itinerary, a shared pace, a shared grade range. Private Coaching exists for the climbers who don't fit that mold: the ones with a specific comp deadline, a very particular technical weakness, a return from injury, or simply a goal that a group format can't move fast enough on.

Every private client works with one dedicated coach who builds a program entirely around them — their goals, their schedule, their location if they want it in-person, or a fully remote arrangement built around filmed sessions and structured feedback. No shared attention, no compromise pace.

Sessions happen wherever suits you: your home crag, your local gym, one of our destination venues, or entirely on video. The constant is the coach — the same person, working the same plan, for as long as the goal takes.

Frequently Asked

Before you book
private coaching

What level do I need to be?

Any level — private coaching works for complete beginners building fundamentals through to competitive athletes chasing a specific result. The program is built around where you're starting from, not a fixed entry grade.

How do the three disciplines work?

Every private program sits under Sport Climbing Private, Multi-Pitch Climbing Private, or Trad Climbing Private — pick the terrain you're working on first, then choose a format (single session, weekend, ongoing remote, and so on) within it.

How does remote coaching actually work?

You film your climbing sessions on your phone and upload them, your coach reviews the footage and sends back structured feedback plus adjustments to your training plan — all on a regular cadence agreed up front, usually weekly or monthly.

Can I choose my coach?

Yes. Tell us your discipline — sport, multi-pitch, or trad — and your goal when you enquire, and we'll match you with a coach whose specialty fits, or you can request someone specific if you already know who you'd like to work with.

Do you travel to me for in-person sessions?

In most regions, yes — our coaches can travel to your home crag or gym for an added travel fee, or you can join them at one of our destination venues. Tell us your location when you enquire and we'll confirm what's possible.
