Who We Are

Guides who trust
their own systems

Trad and multi-pitch climbing asks for a different kind of trust than sport climbing — there's no bolt line to follow, no fixed protection every two meters. Our trad and multi-pitch courses exist to build that trust properly: real gear placement, real anchor building, real rope systems, taught by IFMGA-certified mountain guides who climb this terrain year-round, not just for the season.

Every course moves at the pace of the climber, not the itinerary. Early days are spent on placement and anchor drills at low consequence; later days move onto full multi-pitch routes with the guide mentoring transitions, pacing, and decision-making from the next stance over — building toward the goal of every course: climbing this terrain independently, without a guide.

We cap every course at four climbers per guide. Small enough that everyone gets hands-on time building anchors and leading pitches, not just watching the guide do it.

Frequently Asked

Before you book a
trad or multi-pitch course

Do I need trad experience to join?

No prior trad or multi-pitch experience is required for our fundamentals courses — you should be a confident, independent lead climber on sport routes. Our multi-pitch intensives assume some prior trad placement experience; each course page lists the exact requirement.

Is gear included?

All technical gear — rack, ropes, and anchor-building equipment — is included and supplied by the guide. You'll need your own harness, helmet, and shoes; we can point you toward rental options at most course hubs.

How is this different from a sport climbing holiday?

Sport climbing holidays use bolted routes with quickdraws at a single crag. Trad and multi-pitch courses use natural gear placement and hand-built anchors on longer routes, often multiple pitches high, in bigger and more remote terrain — and are built to teach you to run those systems yourself.

What's the guide-to-climber ratio?

We cap every course at four climbers per guide, and often run two-per-guide on the more technical multi-pitch days, so everyone gets real time building anchors and leading pitches.
