You must bring a sleeping bag and reasonably tight climbing shoes for the trip. Rockbusters Packing List

Core Climbing Gear

•  Climbing Shoes — reasonably tight/technical (bring 2 pairs for intensive climbing)
•  Harness — comfortable and well-fitting
•  Helmet — lightweight and UIAA certified
•  Belay Device — ATC Guide, Reverso, GriGri, etc., with 2 screw-gate carabiners
•  Personal Sling — one 60cm sling with a dedicated locking carabiner

Apparel & Essentials

•  Approach Shoes — comfortable with sticky rubber for hiking to crags
•  Insulation Layer — lightweight down or synthetic jacket for chilly evenings
•  Weather Protection — reliable rain shell/jacket and a warm hat
•  Headlamp — with fresh batteries for evening activities
•  Sleeping Bag — suitable for mountain nights (comfort rating to 5°C/40°F)
•  Backpack — 30L–40L capacity for carrying gear and lunch to climbing sites

Personal Items

•  Water Bottles — two 1-liter bottles or a hydration system
•  Sun Protection — hat, sunglasses, and high SPF sunscreen
•  Personal Medications — including any prescriptions and basic first aid
•  Toiletries — biodegradable soap, toothbrush, toothpaste, etc.
•  Quick-Dry Towel — lightweight and compact
•  Insect Repellent — for evenings at camp

Clothing

•  Climbing Shorts/Pants — durable and flexible
•  T-Shirts/Tank Tops — 3–4 quick-dry shirts for climbing
•  Base Layers — for cooler days and evenings
•  Underwear & Socks — 5–7 pairs, including some wool socks for cooler evenings
•  Swimming Gear — for potential rest-day activities

Extras

•  Climbing Tape — for finger protection
•  Notebook & Pen — for technique notes and climbing journal
•  Camera — for capturing your climbing achievements
•  Portable Charger — for electronic devices
•  Earplugs — for better sleep in shared camping environments
•  Small Repair Kit — needle, thread, duct tape, etc.

Note: We provide most of the technical equipment, but the personal items listed above are essential for your comfort and safety during the camp.
